# M1.21：named reader direct-caller source inventory

## Scope

本切片只消費 M1.18 已命名的 `0x080ac334`／`0x080ac3ac` direct callers；對
呼叫前已知的 r0 literal candidate、stack-buffer candidate 與 runtime/table-derived
candidate 做 caller-local inventory。每個 ROM pointer 最多讀取 `0x100` bytes，遇到
`0x0000` 或 `0x0301` 即停止；沒有全 ROM string/glyph scan，也不產生翻譯 ledger。

```sh
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 -B \
  games/shin-megami-tensei-2/tools/m121_source_inventory.py \
  --rom /path/to/A5TJ.gba \
  --output /private/tmp/smt2-m121-source-inventory.json
```

## Bounded result

- 64-callsite cap 下形成 37 個 caller-local groups；共 58 個 candidate occurrences、
  56 個 unique pointer values。bounded termination metadata 為 58/58 found：
  `zero_0000` 31 筆、`terminator_0301` 27 筆。這些是 consumer-compatible
  candidates，不是已解出的文字表。
- `0x0815bed4` 起由 `0x080ce760`／`0x080cf414` 兩個 caller 分別引用的 15 筆
  candidate 全部以 `0x0301` 結束；它是下一個最值得做 caller/state mapping 的
  bounded family，但 category 語意尚未命名。
- `0x084f*` caller-local candidates、`0x081652*` zero-terminated candidates、
  `0x080095*` early-ROM candidates 與 M1.19 `0x08162b*` family 均保留為分開的
  caller boundaries。M1.19 的 `0x080b52c4` 精確 route family 仍以 M1.20 的 15
  筆 selector mapping 為準；inventory 中額外的 linear candidates 不升格為 routes。
- stack-buffer groups（尤其 large reader 的四個 `sp+0x0c` path）與
  runtime/table-derived groups 分開記錄；callsite 前的 r0 candidate 是
  linear-bounded evidence，不是完整 CFG/data-flow proof。

## Evidence status

- **Confirmed:** inventory scope is limited to named reader direct callers; caller
  boundaries and source candidate addresses/hashes/lengths/termination counts are
  reproducible; raw bytes and decoded text are not emitted。
- **Provisional:** the `0x0815bed4` family is a likely encoded-string category because
  it is consumed by the named reader and has a bounded `0x0301` record run; no
  Unicode identity or semantic category is assigned。
- **Unknown:** natural scene/state selection、main/event/demon/skill/item/system
  category names、pointer-table stride outside the manually bounded runs、codepage、
  controls beyond `0x0000`/`0x0301`、width and reinsertion。

Next minimum slice: inspect only the `0x080ce760`／`0x080cf414` caller state and
literal branches, then establish whether the 15-record `0x0815bed4` family is a
stable category boundary. Runtime natural evidence remains separate because the
headless GDB listener socket is still unavailable; no synthetic state write is used。
